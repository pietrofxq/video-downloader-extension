    Data

    Forgets every detected video and any captured request headers across all tabs. Use the popup's reset button to clear just the current tab.

    Why “Read your data on all websites”?

    The extension watches network requests for video manifests and fetches the video's own segments to repackage them locally. That requires access to the sites you visit. It runs entirely on your machine — there is no backend, no telemetry, and signed tokens in URLs are redacted from logs and never persisted.
